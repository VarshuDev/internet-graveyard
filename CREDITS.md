The **Internet Graveyard** digital archaeology initiative is made possible through the collective preservation work of digital archivists, open-source engineers, and internet historians worldwide.

## <img src="assets/icons/tombstone.svg" width="20" height="20" align="center" /> Digital Preservation &amp; Historical Archives

- **[The Internet Archive (Wayback Machine)](https://archive.org/)**  
  Essential archival preservation of defunct URLs, early landing pages, sunset farewell letters, and digital artifacts that would have otherwise vanished forever.
- **[Wikipedia & The Wikimedia Foundation](https://www.wikimedia.org/)**  
  Public domain historical documentation, acquisition records, corporate liquidation reports, and timeline chronologies.
- **The Open Web Community**  
  Countless forum threads, user archives, and digital preservationists who captured screenshots, memories, and closing notices before the servers powered down.

## <img src="assets/icons/sound.svg" width="20" height="20" align="center" /> Audio Relics &amp; Procedural Synthesis

- **Web Audio API**  
  All sound relics in the **Sounds of the Dead Web Vault** are generated via **100% procedural Web Audio API synthesis** (zero external MP3 dependencies):
  - **AOL Dial-Up Handshake**: DTMF tone generation, carrier modulation, and high-frequency noise bursts.
  - **ICQ "Uh-Oh!" Message Chirp**: Dual-frequency square/sine wave chirps recreating the iconic notification.
  - **MSN Messenger Nudge**: Dual-frequency bass kick and harmonic rattle accompanied by viewport tremor.
  - **Windows 95/98 Startup Chimes**: Multi-oscillator ethereal chord pad inspired by Brian Eno's sound design.
  - **Skype Incoming Call**: Procedural marimba bell arpeggios.
  - **AIM Wooden Latches**: Audio frequency white-noise bursts simulating mechanical latch acoustics.
  - **Club Penguin Night Club**: 8-bit chiptune loop synthesis.
